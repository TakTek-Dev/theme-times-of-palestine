The Times of Palestine is an English-language news site about Palestine: the news of the day, the context behind it, and the stories of the people who live it.

Who we are

We report from Gaza, the West Bank and Jerusalem, and from Palestinian communities abroad.

How to read us

Our mark is three olive leaves, taken from the olive-leaf border of the kufiya. On the site each leaf has a job, and our colours tell you what kind of story you are reading.

  • Red is now.Live coverage and breaking news. When nothing is live, there is no red on the page.
  • Green is context.Explainers, analysis and background: the reading that helps the news make sense.
  • Black is the record.Our reporting, written and checked, and everything that holds the page together.

Our standards

  1. We say how we know.Every report names its sources, or explains why a source cannot be named.
  2. We verify before we publish.Images and claims are checked before they appear. In live coverage, anything not yet verified is labelled as such.
  3. We show photographs as they were taken.Pictures are cropped for size only. We do not alter them.
  4. We keep news and opinion apart.Opinion and analysis are labelled, and set in a different style from news reporting.

The ring of leaves marks our standards across the site: at the end of every report and in our footer.
